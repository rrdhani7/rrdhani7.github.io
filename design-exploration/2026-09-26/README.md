# Portfolio design exploration

Status: awaiting owner selection. Image concepts only; no site implementation changes.

Reviewed Brian Lovin Sites, Shiori, Rafael Conde, Daniel Eden, Marco Cornacchia, and Josh Puckett. Also used previously inspected Brian Lovin and Paco Coursey. Chose restrained directions suited to one published article. Rafael and Marco were not selected because their large visual/project surfaces depend on more content.

All concepts use the same real article and introduction. Portraits are AI mockup representations based on the owner photo, not production photo assets. Use the original supplied photo in implementation.
